# Council round 4: solo, software-only, environmental — which idea, improved how?

Date: 2026-10-04 · Convened by the wayfinder chair after seven research reports (`../research/R1`–`R7`). Advisors may read those files; cite them.

## The decision

Harshdip (NSUT student, Delhi, strong in ML/conformal prediction/VLMs) must pick ONE idea by Oct 8 08:00 for **WeMakeDevs × AWS Environmental Hacks** (Oct 8–11 2026; tracks Air / Heat & Water / Waste & Energy; per-track winner ₹2L + $2k credits; four runners-up; top-10 students get Amazon fast-track interviews; 5,006 registered). Judged on five criteria with **no published weights**: Idea & Impact ("a small problem solved well beats a big one solved vaguely"), Built on AWS (deploy on AWS or use an AWS OSS tool; must be *shown* in the video), Design & usability ("would someone who isn't on your team know what to do with it?"), Execution ("one feature that runs beats five that almost do"), and a ≤3-minute recorded video (no live demo). Repo history must sit inside Oct 8–11. A Builder Center blog wins a separate prize.

**Standing constraints (non-negotiable):** solo build and solo film; **no custom hardware** (a sensor may be *simulated and labelled* only if essential); **no second person** needed on camera. Working rule on staging: the entrant may play two roles on two devices with on-screen labels (staging on two devices is standard practice), but an idea where the entrant is the *natural user* is preferred, and the entrant role-playing a professional whose *judgement* is the product is weakest. The idea must be genuinely environmental with a person whose day changes (not web-performance or cloud-cost in disguise). AI must never be the judge; a deterministic source decides. Every receipt must be able to say "Missed".

**What already died and why (don't resurrect without new evidence):** Saaf Twin (hardware); Right Hour PE (measured K1 backtest: best PE hour barely moves, swap fires 4–6 % of days; needed a real teacher); PE-intensity (AQI-band lookup, self-reported compliance); PulseProof (fan/light too weak on the meter; an app already counts pulses); FrameDebt (weak environmental fit); Cloud Leak, Tap Witness, Smoke Day Forecast (R² 0.69 from persistence alone).

## What the research established (Oct 4 2026)

- **Delhi air is ramping now** (R1): CPCB AQI 166 on Oct 1 (worst October start in 4 years), ~195 at 9 am Oct 4; 13 stations "poor"; forecast 207–215 for Oct 5–6. **GRAP Stage I not yet invoked**; a live invocation during Oct 5–11 is the most likely on-camera event. Hack-week air will likely be PM2.5 40–80, PM10 150–200 (dust-dominated), stubble share ~0–2 % (R6). Stubble, Diwali (Nov 8), Chhath, WFH mandate (Nov 1) and construction bans are all *after* hack week.
- **CPCB station data is stale upstream** (R2): DTU station dead since 2026-06-23; NSIT Dwarka last reading 56 h old; Anand Vihar 31 h. OpenAQ needs a key and lags ~1.5 days. The **daily AQI Bulletin PDF (~17:00 IST) is reliable** and lists per-station AQI and station count. Always-fresh: Open-Meteo CAMS air model, Open-Meteo weather/ERA5 (wind, boundary layer), NASA FIRMS (keyless 24 h CSV). CAQM orders: SPA site, no RSS, surface in news ~21:30. Water and waste feeds: RED. Grid: one live scrape (meritindia). Solar: PVOutput RED.
- **Monitoring trust is the 2026 story** (R1, R3, R7): "Hawa Ka Hisab" (Oct 1 2026): 796 station-hours pinned at AQI 500 vs 256 in 2025; CAQM published no stats Jan–Aug; **only 9/37 stations worked on Diwali 2025** (SC amicus); EnviroCatalysts: 30/39 monitors offline in the 36-h Diwali window, readings stop near 1000 µg/m³; Business Standard: 173 station-hours missing; CAG: many stations breach siting norms; Newslaundry: PWD water tankers spraying at the Jahangirpuri/Anand Vihar monitors. Reddit's biggest post of the season (7.4k): the sprinkler at the Anand Vihar monitor; "purifier says 500 indoors, official says 150"; Sept 20 2026: "I can smell smog but the meter shows low". **No live public tool anywhere logs station availability or recomputes a counterfactual city AQI** (R3, novelty 4/5; weakness: no human-ack loop). GRAP stages are invoked on this very AQI.
- **Schools** (R1, R7): CAQM advisory Sep 10 2026: no physical school sports Nov–Dec; the DoE compressed the Delhi State School Games into October after a students' HC petition, so outdoor meets collide with the AQI ramp. SC (19 Nov 2025) and Delhi HC (20 Nov 2025: DoE "shirking your responsibility"). AES Delhi runs its own AQI 200/300 policy with 100 monitors; **no Indian PE-intensity / hybrid-day / parent-ledger tool exists** (R3 ranks this #1 on novelty × realism, 3.5 / 4.5, but the ack must be role-played).
- **Hotspot dispatcher** (R3, R6): DPCC opened an AI Integrated Command & Control Centre on Oct 2 2026 (cameras + GPS crews on 700+ construction sites). Transient hotspot excess is real (Bhardwaj et al. arXiv 2410.04309: 189 hidden hotspots), **but sprinkling/anti-smog guns have never shown a PM2.5 drop at a Delhi monitor** (Anand Vihar trial: 444 → 509 during spraying). A station-graded sprinkler loop would mostly say "Missed".
- **Dose engine** (R6): EPA EFH Table 6-2 verified; moderate → sedentary cuts inhaled dose ~5×; Delhi naturally-ventilated I/O ≈ 0.97; HEPA −57 %. EPA's TracMyAir already computes activity-weighted dose; UK AirTrack excludes India (R3 novelty 2.5).
- **Routing complaints** (R7): "Should I report them and where" (plastic burning at 1 am), GRAP-4 construction (replies split cops vs HUDA); ₹5,000 fine; Green Delhi app exists but routing is its weak point.
- **Energy** (R7): Delhi PM Surya Ghar portal launched Sep 21 2026; applicants report net-meter waits of 4–10 weeks against DERC's 15-day rule; vendor pricing confusion. 200/400-unit subsidy cliff persists.
- **AWS** (R5): Bedrock works after the Anthropic use-case form (submit before Oct 8); Nova 2 Lite from Mumbai is global-profile only; Claude Haiku 4.5 has an India geo profile; **Nova 2 Sonic now speaks Hindi** but only in US/EU/Tokyo regions; Polly Kajal + Transcribe hi-IN work in Mumbai; Step Functions `waitForTaskToken` and EventBridge Scheduler are free-tier friendly; SES production access within 24 h; **SMS, Connect-to-India, WhatsApp are out**; web push via own VAPID + Amplify PWA is the channel; IoT Device Simulator is deprecated (use a Lambda publisher); Strands Agents 1.57 has `handoff_to_user`; AgentCore is Paid-plan only; a Strands workshop runs at DTU on Oct 10.
- **Event** (R4): deploy on AWS is enough for the gate; judges are mostly DevRel/senior engineers; high scoring projects feature agentic loops, consumer-UI, live URL, architecture diagram, blog. Organisers: "a write-up reads far better than a repository with three commits and no README".
