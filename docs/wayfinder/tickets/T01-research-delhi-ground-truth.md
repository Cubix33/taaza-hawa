## Question

What will Delhi's air, orders, fires, water, waste and energy look like during Oct 8–11 2026, which real feeds will visibly change on camera, and which 2026 frictions are new?

## Resolution (gist)

- **Air is ramping now:** CPCB AQI 129 (Sep 30) → 166 (Oct 1, worst October start in four years) → ~195 at 9 am Oct 4; 13 stations already "poor" (Mundka 281, Narela 254, IIT Delhi 250, Jahangirpuri 243, Bawana 233). AQEWS forecast 207 (Oct 5), 215 (Oct 6). PM10:PM2.5 ≈ 4, so dust-dominated so far.
- **GRAP Stage I not yet invoked** as of Oct 4. A live invocation during Oct 5–11 is the single most likely visible event. CAQM revised GRAP on Sep 28 2026: DG-set curbs and BS-IV-bus entry ban moved to Stage I; C&D sites ≥500 m² must register and remote-monitor; Stage III → hybrid up to Class V; Stage IV → hybrid VI–IX, XI, 50% WFH.
- **Stubble:** ~20 fires/day now (Punjab 52 to Sep 29; IARI-CREAMS 114 events Sep 15–30). Peak is mid-Oct to mid-Nov, after hack week. Burning has shifted to ~17:00 IST to dodge MODIS/VIIRS; FIRMS afternoon counts under-report (ISRO-SAC).
- **Delhi Winter Action Plan 2026-27 (Sep 29):** 50% WFH, construction bans Nov 1–Jan 31, non-BS-VI entry ban, "No PUCC No Fuel" at 500 ANPR pumps, 37 hotspots inspected weekly. All calendar-based from Nov 1; nothing live in hack week.
- **Monitoring integrity is the 2026 story:** Hawa Ka Hisab (Oct 1): 796 station-hours pinned at AQI 500 vs 256 in 2025; CAQM published no stats Jan–Aug; only 9/37 stations worked on Diwali 2025 (SC amicus); CAQM promised 46 new stations (14 in Delhi). Station count quoted as 37/39/44/45 within one week. No public DPCC low-cost-sensor feed exists.
- **Schools:** CAQM advisory Sep 10 2026: no physical sports/competitions Nov–Dec 2026. DoE moved the Delhi State School Games to finish by end-October after a students' HC petition, so outdoor meets are packed into exactly the AQI ramp.
- **Heat:** monsoon withdrew Sep 23; 35–36.5 °C, RH ~21%, winds <10 km/h, ventilation index <6,000 m²/s for another week: stagnation and heat co-occur.
- **Water/waste/energy:** no live Sep–Oct 2026 episode (Yamuna froth, ammonia cuts, landfill fires are off-cycle). Delhi PM Surya Ghar portal launched Sep 21 (up to ₹1.56 lakh subsidy). Diwali Nov 8, Chhath Nov 13–16, both after hack week.
- **Ranked live triggers Oct 8–11:** (1) GRAP Stage I on AQI >200; (2) station-level divergence and stations dropping out of bulletins; (3) AQEWS flipping to "poor"; (4) stubble counts rising with FIRMS undercount; (5) school games colliding with poor AQI; (6) heat + stagnation for outdoor workers.
