## Question

Which deterministic engines and effect sizes can be defended for the candidate directions?

## Resolution (gist)

- **Hotspot excess:** small on average (+6–15 % over city mean in CSE 2024-25), but **transient** excess is real: Bhardwaj et al. (arXiv 2410.04309) found 189 hidden hotspots and a plume model explains 65 % of transient spikes with local sources. Engine: station minus city median, with CO co-rise and low wind.
- **Sprinkling / anti-smog guns:** Delhi's only public trial (Anand Vihar, Dec 2017) showed PM2.5 **444 → 509 µg/m³ during spraying**; the best study (Baoding) shows PM10 −37 to −70 % for ~60 min, back to baseline in ~70 min. **No Delhi PM2.5 effect at any monitor exists.** A dispatcher graded by the station would mostly grade "Missed".
- **Burning signatures:** PM-rich, CO-poor, night BC 6.0 vs day 4.0 µg/m³; CPCB has no BC, and the city-wide 20–24 h peak mimics burning. Biomass share: TERI/ARAI 14 % winter Delhi; waste burning ≈ 8 %.
- **Inhaled dose (EPA EFH Table 6-2, verified):** adult 21–31 y m³/min: sleep 0.0043, sedentary 0.0042, light 0.012, moderate 0.026, high 0.050; child 6–11 y: 0.0045 / 0.0048 / 0.011 / 0.022 / 0.042. **Moderate → sedentary cuts dose ~5×.** Indoor/outdoor PM2.5 in Delhi naturally ventilated homes ≈ 0.97 (dry season); AC roughly halves infiltration; HEPA classroom −57 %; filtered ventilation −95 %.
- **Behaviour-change evidence** is thin: one Scotland N=28 feedback trial (asthma symptom-hours −4.6 % vs +0.5 %), one Bogotá app RCT (self-reported avoidance). None in India; none activity-aware.
- **PV soiling:** RdTools needs weeks of daily data; Delhi soiling 0.3–0.6 %/day; haze −11.5 % insolation/yr (Peters 2018). No paper separates dust from haze on one rooftop with public PM2.5 alone.
- **Classrooms:** CO₂ generation per child 0.0025–0.0040 L/s (Persily & de Jonge); Indian NV classrooms 15.6–61.5 L/s/person; indoor PM > outdoor in Delhi NV schools (Goyal & Khare). No Indian study quantifies the ventilation-vs-smog optimum.
- **Diurnal structure (33 stations, 2017–24):** peaks 08–10 h and 20–24 h, afternoon trough; post-monsoon/winter 300–350 µg/m³.
- **Early-October reality:** 7 Oct 2024 PM2.5 57 / PM10 156 (AQI 167); 8–9 Oct 2025 AQI 81–93. **Oct 8–11 2026 demo will likely see PM2.5 40–80, PM10 150–200, stubble share 0–2 %.** Dust/PM10 and dose engines have signal; burning and stubble demos look quiet.
- **Stubble transport** 14–22 h; IITM DSS share Oct 12–21 2024 = 0.92 %, Oct–Nov 2025 mean 4.2 %, peak 22 % on Nov 12–13. **>90 % of large Punjab fires now after 15:00** (3 % in 2021); MODIS/VIIRS counts and DSS shares are biased low.
