## 2. GRAP 2026

**Invocation status.** No CAQM order invoking Stage I for 2026–27 was found on pib.gov.in, newsonair, ddnews or in Oct 1–4 AQI coverage. Latest GRAP events: Stage IV Jan 18, 2026; Stage III Jan 16–22, 2026; Stage I Apr 16, 2026 (AQI 226). **VERIFIED (absence)**. Careerindia's "Stage 1 active from Oct 1" conflates the revised schedule taking effect with an invocation. caqm.nic.in could not be parsed.

**Revised schedule (CAQM 30th Full Commission, Sep 28, 2026; disclosed Sep 29).** **VERIFIED** (eqmagpro mirror of PIB; ThePrint Sep 29; Careerindia Oct 1):
- Stage I (AQI 201–300): now 25 actions, including two moved up from Stage II — DG-set restrictions (only compliant or retrofitted units; hospitals/lifts exempt — hits RWAs/societies) and an entry ban on BS-IV-and-below inter-city buses (only EV/CNG/BS-VI). Also: C&D sites of 500 m² or more must register on SPCB portals, remote-monitor, run anti-smog guns, wheel-wash; biomass/waste-burning ban with penalties; firecracker court orders enforced; coal/firewood tandoor ban.
- Stage II (301–400): 8 actions, unchanged in substance.
- Stage III (401–450): 10 actions; pothole-repair relaxation; BS-III petrol / BS-IV diesel LMV ban now covers Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, GB Nagar **and Sonipat**; essential-commodity exemptions removed; schools up to Class V hybrid. **VERIFIED** except school clause (REPORTED).
- Stage IV (>450): 5 actions; essential-goods exemption removed; schools VI–IX and XI hybrid; 50% WFH. **REPORTED** for school/WFH.
- New: restrictions on diesel BS-IV-and-below LGV/MGV registered outside Delhi; expanded Citizens' Charter "Do's and Don'ts". **VERIFIED**.
- Construction workers: SC has directed NCR states to pay weekly subsistence from labour-cess funds during Stage III/IV bans; Delhi paid Rs 10,000 per worker in Dec 2025. **REPORTED** (newsonair Dec 17, 2025).

## 3. Stubble burning 2026 so far

- Punjab, to Sep 29: **52** incidents; Amritsar 30, Tarn Taran 8, Ferozepur 5, Gurdaspur 3, Moga 2, one each Fazilka/Hoshiarpur/SBS Nagar/Patiala; physical verification confirmed burning in 16; 1 FIR; Rs 55,000 environmental compensation imposed (Rs 25,000 recovered). **VERIFIED** (Punjab Kesari/IANS, Sep 30). No 2025 comparison given.
- IARI-CREAMS regional count Sep 15–30: **114** events (Punjab 59, UP 32, MP 17, Haryana 3, Rajasthan 2, Delhi 1); 19 on Sep 30; Oct 1: Punjab 4, UP 5, MP 14 (23 total). **VERIFIED** (Jagran/Millennium Post).
- Search snippets citing "877 fires Sep 15–Oct 7" or "Oct 1–14, 2026" are from earlier seasons — ignore.
- Measures: Punjab ~500 more CHCs (rent-free for small farmers); Haryana Rs 1,200/acre CRM incentive; 50%/80% machinery subsidy; "Parali Protection Force" for night fires. **REPORTED**.
- Detection gap (matters for any FIRMS-based demo): ISRO-SAC study (Tribune, Dec 10, 2025; MDPI Remote Sensing 2026) — peak burn time shifted 13:30 → 17:00 IST (2020→2024) to dodge MODIS/VIIRS; VIIRS trend −18,883 counts/yr vs Meteosat SEVIRI +43/yr. Afternoon polar-orbit counts under-report. **VERIFIED**.
