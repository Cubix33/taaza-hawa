Thirty-six green tests, one wrong clock

The clock field we trusted was 5 h 30 min off

In the WAQI payloads we checked, time.v encoded the local wall clock as an epoch. Treating it as UTC made Delhi readings look younger.

"time": {
  "s":   "2026-10-09 06:00:00",
  "tz":  "+05:30",
  "v":   1791525600,
  "iso": "2026-10-09T06:00:00+05:30"
}
// v decodes to 06:00 UTC = 11:30 IST
// iso says 06:00 IST     = 00:30 UTC
Same shape as WAQI's public demo feed, checked on 9 Oct 2026 (Shanghai: v = 14:00 "UTC", iso = 14:00+08:00).
